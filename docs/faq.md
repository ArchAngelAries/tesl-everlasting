# FAQ — TESL: Everlasting

**Is this the official game?**
No. It's a free fan project that lets the original game run offline. It's not affiliated with or endorsed by Bethesda,
ZeniMax, Dire Wolf Digital, Sparkypants or TESL-Reborn.

**Do you include the game?**
No. We don't distribute any game files. You need your own installed copy. We won't explain how to get the game if you
can't get it officially; see the [DISCLAIMER](../DISCLAIMER.md).

**Do I need TESL-Reborn?**
No. TESL: Everlasting works on its own, with no account, internet or server. If you want to play against other people,
[TESL-Reborn](https://tesl-reborn.com) is a separate community project for that, and the two coexist (F10 switches mode).

**Can I play against my friends with it?**
Not yet. TESL: Everlasting is offline against the in-game AI. For human-vs-human play, use TESL-Reborn. A self-hostable
community server for modded multiplayer (everyone with the same mods) is a long-term idea.

**Can I use cheats or mods online?**
Cheats and gameplay mods: never. Local visual and audio mods (remastered art, sounds, music, boards only you see) and
graphics options: yes. A client that tries to use offline code in a remote match is closed by the fair-play check.

**Can an AI agent or LLM play for me online?**
No. AI agent and LLM players are for offline play against the in-game AI only. No cheating by humans or by AI.

**Where is my progress saved?**
In the game folder, `.offline-user-data/profile.json`. Back that folder up.

**Can I carry over my old online collection?**
No. Bethesda's servers held that data and they're gone. You can unlock everything with the F10 cheats if you want to.

**Which stories work?**
The Forgotten Hero, The Dark Brotherhood, Clockwork City and Isle of Madness (all 187 battles start), plus any story mods.

**Are all cards working?**
Every card in the game (1,718) has its full rules.

**Will it break my game?**
It doesn't change game files. Remove the `TESLOffline` folders and the game is as it was. Back up `.offline-user-data`.

**Mac, Linux, Steam Deck, mobile, consoles?**
Windows only for now. The game is Windows-only on PC; Linux/Steam Deck with Proton may work but is untested.

**Can I sell mods I make?**
Yes, your own creations are yours (see [Licensing](LICENSING.md)). Selling TESL: Everlasting or TESL Forge themselves is
not allowed. Material that belongs to Bethesda stays theirs.

**How do I report a bug?**
Use this project's issue tracker with the files from `.offline-user-data\logs` (and `BepInEx/LogOutput.log`) and steps to reproduce. Never report it to TESL-Reborn.

**How can I help?**
Play, report bugs, make mods, and if you like, support us on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/archangelaries) or
[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/ArchAngelAries).
